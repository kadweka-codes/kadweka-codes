### 👨‍💻 Executive Summary

I am a **Full-Stack Software Engineer** and **Enterprise ERP Specialist** based in **Kenya 🇰🇪**, specializing in architecting scalable backend systems, customizing end-to-end **Odoo ERP** workflows, and building high-performance **cross-platform mobile applications**. 

With hands-on experience in **FinTech and core banking environments** (including work at **FinAccess**), I bridge the gap between complex business logic, regulatory compliance, and intuitive user interfaces. Whether designing custom PostgreSQL-backed Odoo modules, integrating payment gateways and tax compliance APIs (e.g., KRA eTIMS/ESD), or shipping React Native apps, I focus on clean architecture, maintainability, and measurable business impact.

- 🔭 **Currently Working On:** Enterprise Odoo ERP customizations, automated financial workflows, and scalable Node.js/Python microservices.
- 🌱 **Deepening Expertise In:** Distributed systems architecture, Kubernetes orchestration, and event-driven APIs.
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Odoo (ORM, OWL framework, POS, Accounting, Inventory), Python, React Native, Node.js, PostgreSQL optimization, and Docker deployments.
- 🤝 **Open To:** Technical consulting, enterprise ERP architecture roles, FinTech collaborations, and open-source contributions.
